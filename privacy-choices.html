Privacy choices

Use the options below to ask a privacy question, request access or deletion, or manage the Apple services connected to Puzzler.

Make a request

Email us to request access, correction, or deletion of information we control.

Manage subscriptions

View, change, or cancel Puzzler subscriptions through Apple.

What to include

Please describe your request and include enough information for us to respond. If your request concerns a support conversation, email us from the same address you originally used. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

Analytics and diagnostics

You can review Apple’s analytics-sharing controls under Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Puzzler’s analytics records are not linked by us to your name. If records are associated only with a random device or installation identifier, we may not be able to locate a particular historical record.

Game Center

Manage Game Center through your device settings and Apple Account. You can sign out of Game Center or adjust profile and friend settings there.

Response timing

We will acknowledge your request and respond within the period required by applicable law. We may need to verify your request to protect your information. Some records may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, or transaction purposes.
