Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Puzzler’s settings or purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Choose Puzzler to view or change your subscription. Apple manages all billing and cancellations.

My purchase is not unlocking. What should I do?

First try Restore Purchases and confirm you have an internet connection. If it still does not unlock, email us with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Puzzler version, and the name of the pack or subscription. Never send full payment details.

How do leaderboards work?

Puzzler uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards. Sign in to Game Center in device settings, then complete a timed puzzle to submit an eligible score.

How can I report a bug?

Email us with what happened, what you expected, and steps that reproduce the problem. Your device model, operating-system version, app version, and an optional screenshot are especially useful.
