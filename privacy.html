Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Spencer Hall (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Puzzler: Sort and Match Tiles (“Puzzler”).

Information we collect

Usage and diagnostic data

Puzzler and its analytics provider, Amplitude, may collect product interaction data such as screens viewed, game types selected, difficulty and sort-order choices, hints or reveals used, game completion information, purchase-result status, and general app interactions. We may also receive diagnostic information such as crash data, performance data, app version, device type, operating-system version, and a randomly generated device or installation identifier.

We use this information to understand how Puzzler is used, improve gameplay and reliability, diagnose problems, and measure the effectiveness of features. This data is not used for third-party advertising and is not linked by us to your name or contact information.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock purchased content, restore purchases, and understand whether a purchase succeeded, failed, or was cancelled. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy policy.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, Apple may provide Puzzler with the information needed to authenticate you, submit scores, display leaderboards, and award achievements. Game Center is operated by Apple and is subject to Apple’s privacy practices and your Apple Account settings.

Information you send us

If you contact support, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, screenshots, and device or app details. We use it only to respond, troubleshoot, and improve Puzzler.

How information is collected

Information is collected automatically when you use Puzzler, through Apple services you choose to use, or directly from you when you contact us. Puzzler does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo library.

Sharing and service providers

We share information only as needed with service providers that help operate Puzzler:

  • Amplitude, for product analytics, diagnostics, and app improvement.
  • Apple, for App Store purchases, subscriptions, receipt and entitlement processing, Game Center, app distribution, and platform diagnostics.

We require service providers handling information on our behalf to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Retention and deletion

We retain analytics and diagnostic data only as long as reasonably necessary to understand app use, maintain security and reliability, and improve Puzzler. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve your request and maintain appropriate business records. Purchase records may be retained by Apple and by us as necessary to provide entitlements, meet accounting obligations, prevent fraud, and comply with law.

You may request deletion of information we control by visiting Privacy Choices or emailing spencer.hall.31@gmail.com. Because analytics data is not linked by us to your name and may be associated only with a random identifier, we may not always be able to identify and delete a specific historical record. We will explain any limitation in our response.

Your choices

  • You can choose not to use Game Center or change Game Center settings in your Apple Account and device settings.
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • You can limit the sharing of analytics and diagnostics with app developers through your device’s Privacy & Security settings where Apple provides those controls.
  • You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you have provided directly to us.

Children

Puzzler is rated 4+ and is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through Puzzler. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

International processing

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. Those locations may have different data-protection laws than your country. We and our providers use safeguards required by applicable law.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Puzzler or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date. Material changes will be communicated as appropriate.
